## Required evidence

- Signed contractor and employment agreements containing appropriate IP provisions.
- Separate deed or written assignment where historic terms are missing or unclear.
- Invoices, statements of work and acceptance records.
- Git commit history and pull-request records.
- Dated specifications, mock-ups, diagrams and product documentation.
- Records showing which legal entity paid for and commissioned work.

## Third-party and open-source components

Maintain an inventory containing package name, version, licence, copyright holder, source, use within AlistraGIS, required notices and any distribution obligations. No package should be introduced without a licence review proportionate to the risk.

## AI-assisted development

Record material AI-assisted outputs, the service used, the human review performed and whether confidential or customer data was submitted. AI output must be reviewed for security, originality, licensing risk and compatibility with project standards.

## Ownership statement for agreements

Customer contracts should distinguish clearly between:

1. AlistraGIS background IP, platform code, APIs, reusable modules, methods and know-how.
2. Customer data and customer-provided materials.
3. Bespoke deliverables, including whether they are assigned, licensed exclusively or licensed non-exclusively.
4. Third-party software and services governed by their own terms.
