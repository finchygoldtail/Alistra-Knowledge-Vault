# Copyright Notice Standard

## Approved notice template

Use the following after the legal owner has been confirmed:

`© [first publication year]–[current year] [legal owner]. All rights reserved.`

Product wording may be added where useful:

`AlistraGIS and its software, interface designs, documentation and original content are protected by copyright and other intellectual-property rights. Except as permitted by an applicable written licence, no part may be copied, modified, distributed, reverse engineered or used to create a competing product.`

A solicitor should review final wording and ownership before publication.

## Where notices should appear

- Website footer and website terms.
- AlistraGIS application footer or About screen.
- User manuals, API documentation and commercial proposals.
- Source-code repository README files where appropriate.
- Generated reports and exported documents where commercially appropriate.
- Marketing artwork and downloadable resources.

## Source-file headers

Do not automatically insert long copyright headers into every file. Use a repository-level notice unless legal advice, customer requirements or a specific distribution model requires file-level headers.

Suggested short header where appropriate:

`Copyright © [year] [legal owner]. Licensed for use only under the applicable AlistraGIS agreement.`

## Third-party notices

AlistraGIS copyright notices must not remove or replace third-party notices. Maintain a `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES` or equivalent record for components that require attribution, licence text or source-code offers.

## Brand symbols

- Use `™` to indicate a claimed brand where appropriate.
- Use `®` only once the relevant trade mark is registered and only in connection with the registered goods/services and territory.
