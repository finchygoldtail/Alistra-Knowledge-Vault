# AlistraGIS Trade Mark Registration Plan

> This is a filing-preparation record, not a legal opinion that a mark is available or registrable. Obtain advice from a UK trade mark attorney if clearance identifies a similar mark or ownership is uncertain.

## Recommended UK-first strategy

Prepare **two separate applications**:

1. **Word mark:** `ALISTRAGIS` — protects the name independently of font, colour or logo treatment.
2. **Figurative mark:** the final approved AlistraGIS logo — protects the exact visual composition filed.

File the word mark first if budget requires prioritisation. The logo contains the wording `ALISTRA GIS`, so clearance must cover both the joined and spaced versions. Do not combine the word mark and materially different logo into a series application without professional advice; UKIPO says series differences must be minor and notes that around 40% of series applications do not meet the requirements.

The possible secondary word mark `ALISTRA` should be cleared separately. It may offer useful protection for the distinctive part of the brand, but it would require another application and may encounter a wider range of earlier marks.

## Clearance search — mandatory before submission

Use the official [UK trade mark search](https://www.gov.uk/search-for-trademark) and search relevant classes for:

- [ ] `ALISTRAGIS`
- [ ] `ALISTRA GIS`
- [ ] `ALISTRA`
- [ ] `ALISTR*`
- [ ] close phonetic spellings such as `ALYSTRA`, `ALISTERA` and `ALYSTRA GIS`
- [ ] similar logo/device marks using the official image search

For every potentially relevant result, record the mark, number, owner, status, classes, terms and a short visual/phonetic/conceptual assessment in [[Trade Mark Clearance Search Record]]. Search Companies House, domains, app stores and the wider GIS, mapping, telecoms and field-service software market as supporting checks; these do not replace the UK register search.

Do not file without professional advice if an identical or similar earlier mark covers overlapping goods or services. UKIPO may notify owners of earlier similar marks, but that is not a substitute for applicant clearance.

## Candidate classes and scope

Protection only covers the classes and terms selected. UKIPO says to choose goods and services relevant to genuine business plans for the next five years; terms cannot be added after submission.

### Core candidate — Class 42

Likely the primary class for the hosted platform:

- Software as a service (SaaS) featuring software for fibre-network planning, mapping, field-service delivery, project management, asset management, workflow management, reporting and data analysis.
- Platform as a service (PaaS) featuring software platforms for fibre-network delivery and geospatial data management.
- Design, development, hosting, maintenance and updating of computer software.
- Providing temporary use of online non-downloadable software for geographic information, network planning, field operations and infrastructure asset management.

### Conditional candidate — Class 9

Include only if downloadable software or mobile applications are or will credibly be supplied within five years:

- Downloadable computer software and mobile applications for fibre-network planning, geographic information, field-service delivery, project management, asset management and reporting.
- Downloadable software for data collection, mapping, workflow management and offline field operations.

### Add only if separately commercialised

- **Class 41:** paid training or education services supplied under the brand.
- **Class 35:** business-management, data-processing or contractor-management services supplied as services in their own right, rather than merely functions of the software.

Validate every final term in the official [UK trade mark classes search](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/search-uk-trade-mark-classes) or with the UKIPO Classification Team/trade mark attorney. Avoid broad class headings and services AlistraGIS does not genuinely plan to offer.

## Cost model — UKIPO fees at 5 September 2026

| Route | One class, per mark | Two classes, per mark | Both word and logo in one class each | Both word and logo in two classes each |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Standard online | £205 | £265 | £410 | £530 |
| Right Start, total if continued | £250 | £310 | £500 | £620 |

Right Start is paid in two equal stages: £125 plus £30 for each additional class for the initial check, then the same amount to continue. The applicant has 28 days to decide whether to continue. Fees are generally not recovered simply because an application encounters problems, so clearance and specification review should happen first.

Professional search, drafting or representation fees are additional.

## Expected UK timeline

If the application is not objected to or opposed:

1. UKIPO examination is normally within about two weeks.
2. An accepted application is published for a two-month opposition period.
3. Registration normally follows about two weeks after that period.
4. Overall registration is typically around three months from application.
5. Registration lasts ten years and can be renewed every ten years.

Record all actual deadlines from UKIPO correspondence; do not rely solely on this indicative timeline.

## Post-filing controls

- Use ™ where appropriate while registration is pending; use ® only after registration and for the registered mark.
- Monitor new UK applications and confusingly similar names, domains and products.
- Ensure contracts and licences identify the trade mark owner and permitted brand use.
- Record any ownership/address changes with UKIPO.
- Calendar the ten-year renewal deadline well in advance.
- Review whether EU or other international protection is commercially justified; a UK registration does not provide EU-wide protection.
