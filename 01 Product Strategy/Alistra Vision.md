# Alistra Vision

AlistraGIS is a fibre delivery operating platform for planning, map-led production, audits, work packs, PIA, permits, QA, H&S, handover, BOQ, stock and reporting.

It should give office and field teams one shared view of delivery progress while keeping permissions clear through [[Permissions Model]] and workflows practical through [[Mobile and Tablet Plan]].
