# Platform Scope

## In Scope

- Multi-company and multi-project support through [[Multi-Tenant Architecture]].
- Mapping with Leaflet and react-leaflet through [[Main Map]].
- Firebase Authentication, Firestore and Storage through [[Firebase Infrastructure]].
- Production, audits, work packs, PIA, permits, QA, H&S, handover, BOQ, stock and reporting.
- Plant & equipment register, vehicle register and crew register, added 2026-08-06 — see [[Plant and Equipment]], [[Vehicle Management]], [[Work Packs]].
- Employee credential/accreditation roster, a Supervisor role tier, single-session login enforcement, vehicle QR-scan checkout, and as-built document generation from live map state, added 2026-08-07 — see [[Employee Credentials]], [[Role Matrix]], [[Concurrent Login Control]], [[Vehicle Management]], [[As-Built Generation]].

## Out of Scope Until Defined

- External client API buyout terms. See [[API Buyout]].
- Client-hosted deployment detail. See [[Client Hosted Deployment]].
