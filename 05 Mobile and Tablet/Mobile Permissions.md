# Mobile Permissions

Mobile permissions must follow [[Permissions Model]] and keep restricted actions out of field workflows unless explicitly assigned.

## Rules

- Managers and users cannot draw.
- Managers and users may navigate, measure and toggle layers.
- Drawing must remain admin or super-admin controlled.
