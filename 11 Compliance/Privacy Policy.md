# Privacy Policy

> Draft for legal review before publication.

## Who We Are
AlistraGIS provides mapping, fibre-network, project-workspace, audit and operational-management software. The contracting entity and contact details must be inserted before publication.

## Information We Process
Depending on the deployment, we may process account details, business contact information, role and organisation membership, project activity, support communications, audit records, device and security logs, uploaded documents, photographs and location-related operational data.

## Why We Use It
We use information to provide and secure the service, authenticate users, manage permissions, support customers, maintain auditability, prevent abuse, improve reliability, meet contractual duties and comply with law.

## Legal Bases
Applicable bases may include contract, legitimate interests, legal obligation and consent where genuinely optional. The correct basis must be documented for each processing activity and deployment model.

## Sharing
Information may be processed by hosting, authentication, storage, monitoring, communications and support suppliers, and by customer-selected infrastructure providers. Subprocessors must be documented and contractually controlled.

## International Transfers
Where information is transferred outside the UK, appropriate safeguards and transfer-risk assessments will be used where required.

## Retention
We retain information only for defined business, contractual and legal periods, then delete or anonymise it under the Data Retention Policy.

## Your Rights
Subject to applicable law, individuals may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection or withdrawal of consent. Identity may be verified before action.

## Security
We use access controls, encryption, logging, testing, backups and incident procedures designed to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## Contact and Complaints
Insert the privacy contact and registered business details. Individuals may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

## Changes
Material changes will be dated and communicated through an appropriate channel.
