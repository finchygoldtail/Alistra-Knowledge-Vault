- [ ] Obtain solicitor or trade-mark attorney advice before filing where conflicts exist.

## Likely commercial coverage to discuss with an adviser

The final specification must accurately describe the real products and services. Potential areas include:

- Downloadable and recorded software.
- SaaS and hosted software services.
- API and platform services.
- GIS, mapping, telecom-network planning and asset-management software.
- Software development, implementation, support and consultancy.
- Training and technical documentation where commercially relevant.

Do not file overly broad classes merely to reserve them. The filing strategy should reflect current use and credible planned use.

## Post-filing controls

- Keep the filing certificate and correspondence in this vault.
- Record opposition deadlines and renewal dates.
- Use ™ while an application is pending where appropriate; use ® only after registration and only for the registered mark.
- Monitor confusingly similar names, domains and products.
- Ensure licences clearly state who owns the mark and how customers may use it.
