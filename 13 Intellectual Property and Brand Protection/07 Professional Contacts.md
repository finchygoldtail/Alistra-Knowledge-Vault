## Due-diligence checklist before appointment

- [ ] Verify the individual and firm through official professional registers.
- [ ] Confirm experience with UK software, SaaS, API licensing and trade marks.
- [ ] Request written scope, fees, exclusions and expected timescale.
- [ ] Confirm who will own and control any trade-mark application account.
- [ ] Ask for conflict checks before disclosing sensitive information.
- [ ] Use the NDA where appropriate before sharing source code or commercial plans.
- [ ] Retain engagement letters, advice and invoices in a restricted legal folder.
