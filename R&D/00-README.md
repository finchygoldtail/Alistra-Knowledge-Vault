## Current strongest candidate areas

1. Scalable real-time GIS rendering and data storage.
2. Fibre-network topology and continuity modelling.
3. Multi-tenant isolation and configurable client storage.
4. Secure API modularisation of the existing platform.
5. Geospatial import and normalisation across GeoJSON, KML, KMZ and GPKG.
6. Mobile/tablet GIS performance and interaction.
7. Append-only audit integrity and concurrent-session control.
8. AI-assisted fibre-network engineering.

## Evidence rules

Every major entry should record:

- date or period;
- people involved;
- technological baseline;
- advance sought;
- uncertainty encountered;
- approaches attempted;
- failures and changes of direction;
- outcome and remaining uncertainty;
- links to commits, screenshots, tests, invoices or deployment records;
- approximate qualifying and non-qualifying time.

## Company boundary

AlistraGIS Ltd was incorporated in late July 2026. Pre-incorporation technical history should remain documented, but costs incurred personally before the company existed must be separated from company-incurred expenditure and reviewed professionally.
