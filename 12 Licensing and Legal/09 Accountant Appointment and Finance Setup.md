## 5. Unit economics to report monthly

| Metric | Calculation |
|---|---|
| Annual recurring revenue | Contracted recurring subscription value |
| Monthly recurring revenue | ARR ÷ 12, adjusted for actual terms |
| Gross margin | Revenue less direct service-delivery costs |
| Hosting cost per active customer | Allocated cloud and mapping cost ÷ active customers |
| Cost per active user | Allocated variable cost ÷ active users |
| Support cost per customer | Support time and tools allocated per customer |
| Customer acquisition cost | Sales and marketing spend ÷ new customers |
| Cash runway | Available cash ÷ average monthly net cash outflow |

## 6. Contract and invoice controls

Every customer record should contain:

- legal customer name and billing address;
- signed Order Form and applicable terms version;
- licence type, users, hosting model and renewal date;
- implementation and recurring charges separated;
- VAT treatment and currency;
- purchase-order requirements;
- payment terms, overdue process and credit limit;
- variable usage and overage evidence;
- cancellation and renewal notice dates.

## 7. Accountant questions for the four commercial models

### Outright sale

Confirm tax, VAT, IP-disposal treatment, warranties, retained rights, payment structure and whether the transaction is an asset sale or wider business transfer.

### Annual licence with maintenance and upgrades

Confirm deferred-income and revenue-recognition treatment, VAT timing and allocation between implementation and recurring service.

### Employment or reduced-price arrangement

Confirm whether any reduction, IP use or compensation forms taxable remuneration, benefit, licence income or a connected-party transaction.

### Revenue-share or reseller model

Confirm principal-versus-agent treatment, VAT invoicing, commission recognition, bad-debt ownership and reporting obligations.

## 8. Records policy

Store invoices, receipts, contracts, bank records, payroll, expenses, tax filings, asset records and supporting calculations for the required statutory periods. Access should be restricted and backups tested. The accountant should confirm exact retention periods and any sector-specific requirements.
