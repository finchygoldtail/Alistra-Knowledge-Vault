# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

> **Draft for solicitor review — complete party details and permitted purpose before use.**

**Party A:** [LEGAL NAME AND ADDRESS]  
**Party B:** [LEGAL NAME AND ADDRESS]  
**Effective date:** [DATE]  
**Permitted purpose:** evaluating, negotiating or performing a potential or actual business relationship concerning AlistraGIS and related services.

## 1. Confidential information

Confidential Information means non-public commercial, technical, operational, financial, legal or security information disclosed in any form, including software, source code, architecture, APIs, product plans, pricing, customer information, network designs, credentials, security findings, data models, documents and know-how.

## 2. Obligations

Each receiving party must:

- use Confidential Information only for the Permitted Purpose;
- protect it using at least reasonable care;
- disclose it only to personnel and professional advisers who need to know and are bound by equivalent duties;
- not copy or reverse engineer it except as needed for the Permitted Purpose;
- promptly report suspected unauthorised access or disclosure;
- remain responsible for breaches by its representatives.

## 3. Exclusions

Information is not confidential to the extent the receiving party can show it:

- was lawfully known without restriction before disclosure;
- becomes public without breach;
- is independently developed without use of the disclosed information;
- is lawfully received from a third party without confidentiality duty;
- is approved for release in writing.

## 4. Required disclosure

A receiving party may disclose information where legally required, but where lawful must give prompt notice, disclose only what is required and reasonably assist with protective measures.

## 5. Ownership and no licence

All disclosed information remains the disclosing party's property. No intellectual-property licence or commitment to proceed with a transaction is granted except the limited right to evaluate the information for the Permitted Purpose.

## 6. Personal data and security

Personal data must be handled only as permitted by applicable data-protection law and any separate Data Processing Agreement. Credentials, vulnerability details and security architecture must receive enhanced protection and must not be placed in unapproved systems.

## 7. Return and deletion

On written request or when discussions end, the receiving party must return or securely delete Confidential Information, subject to lawful retention, routine backups and professional record-keeping. Retained material remains protected.

## 8. Duration

This agreement begins on the Effective Date. Confidentiality obligations continue for [five] years after the last disclosure. Trade secrets and source code remain protected for as long as they retain confidential or trade-secret status.

## 9. Remedies

The parties acknowledge that unauthorised disclosure may cause harm not adequately remedied by damages alone and that the affected party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief, in addition to available remedies.

## 10. No warranty

Information is provided for evaluation without warranty as to accuracy or completeness, except where expressly agreed in a later binding contract.

## 11. General

This agreement is the entire agreement on confidentiality for the Permitted Purpose. Changes must be in writing and signed. Invalid provisions are severed to the minimum extent necessary. No third party has enforcement rights.

## 12. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
