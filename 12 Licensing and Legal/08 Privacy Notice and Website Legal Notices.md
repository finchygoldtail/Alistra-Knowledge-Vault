Customers must not use AlistraGIS to collect unnecessary special-category or criminal-offence data without documented legal, security and contractual controls.

## 4. Customer-controlled data

For project, employee, contractor, network, audit or operational data uploaded by a customer, the customer will usually determine the purposes and means of processing.

Questions and rights requests about customer-controlled data should normally be directed to the relevant customer/controller. AlistraGIS will assist the customer according to the Data Processing Agreement and applicable law.

We may share personal data with approved providers used for:

- optional AI functionality where contractually enabled;

- authorised customers/users where necessary to provide the service.

The current provider/subprocessor position is maintained in [[Subprocessor Register]]. A public or customer-accessible version should be made available before ordinary commercial launch.

## 6. International transfers

Where personal data is processed outside the UK, AlistraGIS must identify and document the lawful transfer mechanism where required, including relevant adequacy regulations, UK IDTA/Addendum or other permitted safeguard.

Do not publish an absolute UK-only/EU-only statement until the actual regions and provider chains are verified. Current technical records have previously identified `europe-west2` Firestore and some US-region Storage/backup processing requiring confirmation.

## 7. Retention

Personal data is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, legal obligations, dispute handling, security and contractual commitments.

Detailed categories and approved periods belong in [[Data Retention Schedule]]. Backups may retain deleted data for a limited protected cycle before expiry. Application configuration must not be treated as effective retention unless enforcement has been implemented or an operational process exists.

## 8. Security

AlistraGIS uses proportionate organisational and technical controls which may include:

- authentication and role-based access;
- tenant/business scoping;
- server-side authorisation for privileged operations;
- encryption in transit and provider-managed encryption at rest where applicable;
- restricted administration and secret management;
- security/audit logging;
- secure development/testing;
- backup/restore controls;
- incident-response procedures.

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Individual rights

Depending on the circumstances, individuals may have rights including:

- access;
- rectification;
- erasure;
- restriction;
- objection;
- portability where applicable;
- rights relating to automated decision-making; and
- withdrawal of consent where consent is relied upon.

Requests may be sent to **Alistair.Grantham@alistragis.com** or another published privacy channel.

For subject access requests, AlistraGIS/controller should normally respond without undue delay and within **one month**, subject to the current rules on identity, authorised representatives, clarification, permitted extensions and exemptions. See [[Data Subject Rights Procedure]].

Identity and authority may be verified where reasonably necessary before personal data is disclosed, altered or erased.

## 10. Data-protection complaints

AlistraGIS maintains [[12 Data Protection Complaints Procedure]].

A person can raise a complaint about how their personal data is handled through the published privacy contact or another reasonable complaint route.

AlistraGIS will:

- provide a clear complaint route;
- acknowledge a data-protection complaint within **30 days of receipt**;
- take appropriate steps to investigate without undue delay;
- keep the complainant appropriately informed; and
- communicate the outcome without undue delay.

Where the complaint concerns customer-controlled data, AlistraGIS may need to involve the relevant customer/controller while still handling any part for which AlistraGIS is itself controller.

Individuals may also raise concerns with the UK Information Commissioner's Office and may have a right to seek a judicial remedy.

## 11. Children

The business service is not intended for children. AlistraGIS must not knowingly create child-user accounts or design processing for children without a specific lawful product/privacy assessment.

## 12. Automated and AI-assisted processing

Describe only AI/automated features that are actually enabled for the relevant customer.

The current FRIDAY concept should not be described as making binding safety, employment, legal or other significant decisions. Operational, safety, employment or legal decisions must not be based solely on unreliable automated output.

AI chat history/RAG, write-capable agents, profiling or significant automated decisions require reassessment of privacy, security, provider terms and safeguards before production enablement.

# Part B — Cookie and Device Storage Notice

The current technical/legal audit records authentication/session/local browser storage used for core service functionality and no active advertising, behavioural marketing or analytics tracker requiring a generic consent banner.

Essential/strictly necessary technologies may be used for authentication, security, load balancing, consent storage and core functionality without deploying a blanket "accept all" banner merely for appearance.

Before adding analytics, advertising, behavioural tracking or any new non-essential storage/access technology:

1. identify the provider and technology;
2. record purpose and duration;
3. assess whether consent is required under the current PECR/DUAA rules;
4. prevent consent-requiring technology from operating before valid consent;
5. make reject/withdraw controls as easy as acceptance where required; and
6. update this notice and the subprocessor/data map as appropriate.

| Provider/technology | Purpose | Type | Duration | Consent required? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Firebase/Auth/browser session state | Authentication/security/core operation | Cookie/local/device storage as configured | Verify live configuration | Normally essential — verify |

| Future analytics/marketing | Not currently approved | TBD | TBD | Assess before enablement |
