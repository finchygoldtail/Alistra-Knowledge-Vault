# Contractor Agreement

> **Draft for UK solicitor and tax-status review.** The written terms must reflect the real working arrangement. Do not rely on the label “contractor” alone when assessing employment status or off-payroll rules.

**Company:** [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME]

**Services:** [DESCRIPTION]  
**Start and end:** [DATES OR TERMINATION BASIS]

## 2. Appointment and status

The Contractor is engaged as an independent contractor to perform the Services and has no authority to bind the Company. Nothing creates employment, worker, agency, partnership or joint-venture status. The Contractor is responsible for taxes, National Insurance, VAT, insurance and statutory obligations arising from its business, subject to mandatory law and any applicable status determination.

## 3. Services and standards

The Contractor will perform the Services with reasonable care, skill and diligence; meet agreed specifications and milestones; follow lawful site, security, health-and-safety and data-protection requirements; and promptly disclose risks, conflicts and delays.

## 4. Control, location and substitution

Working arrangements must match the agreed status assessment. Subject to delivery, security and site requirements, the Contractor controls how the Services are performed. Any right of substitution, if intended, must be genuine, documented and subject only to reasonable competence, security and confidentiality checks.

## 5. Fees and expenses

Fees: [RATE/FIXED PRICE]. Invoices are payable within [30] days after acceptance and receipt of a valid invoice. Expenses require prior written approval and evidence. The Contractor may not commit the Company to expenditure.

## 6. Equipment and access

The Contractor provides its own normal business equipment unless the schedule states otherwise. Company accounts, credentials, devices and data remain Company property, may be monitored for legitimate security purposes and must be returned or disabled at the end of the engagement.

## 7. Intellectual property

To the extent legally possible, all intellectual-property rights in deliverables created specifically for the Services are assigned to the Company on creation or, where needed, on full payment. The Contractor will sign further documents required to confirm ownership.

Pre-existing tools, libraries and background IP remain with the Contractor only if listed in the schedule. The Contractor grants the Company a perpetual, worldwide, transferable, sublicensable, royalty-free licence to any approved background IP embedded in deliverables to use, modify, maintain and commercialise those deliverables.

The Contractor must not introduce third-party or open-source material without approval and licence disclosure.

## 8. Confidentiality

The Contractor must protect Company and customer confidential information, including AlistraGIS source code, APIs, architecture, credentials, vulnerability information, pricing, plans and network data. These obligations survive termination.

## 9. Data protection and security

The Contractor may process personal data only on documented instructions, using approved systems and minimum necessary access. Incidents, loss of equipment or suspected compromise must be reported immediately. Data must not be copied to personal storage or unapproved AI services.

## 10. Warranties

The Contractor warrants that deliverables are original or properly licensed, do not knowingly infringe third-party rights, are free from malicious code, and comply materially with the agreed specification. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed.

## 11. Insurance

The Contractor must maintain appropriate professional indemnity, public liability, employers' liability where applicable, and cyber insurance at levels stated in the schedule, and provide evidence on request.

## 12. Termination

Either party may terminate on [NOTICE]. The Company may terminate immediately for serious breach, dishonesty, security misconduct, unlawful conduct, repeated failure, insolvency or material confidentiality/IP breach. The Contractor must provide an orderly handover and return all property.

## 13. Liability and indemnity

Liability, exclusions and any indemnities must be agreed with a solicitor and proportionate to fees, insurance and risk. Proposed cap: [AMOUNT/MULTIPLE OF FEES], subject to separate treatment for fraud, deliberate breach, confidentiality, data protection and IP infringement.

## 14. Restrictions

Any non-solicitation or non-dealing restriction must be narrowly drafted and solicitor-approved. Proposed restriction: for [6] months, the Contractor will not knowingly solicit Company personnel or customers with whom the Contractor had material dealings, except through general advertising.

## 15. Law

England and Wales law and courts apply.
