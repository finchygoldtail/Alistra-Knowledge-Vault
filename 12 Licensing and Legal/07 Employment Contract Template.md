# Employment Contract Template

> **Draft for UK employment-law review.** This template must be adapted for each role and accompanied by current policies. Employers must provide the required principal written statement on or before the first day of work and remaining prescribed particulars within the legal timeframe.

## 1. Parties

**Employer:** [LEGAL NAME, COMPANY NUMBER, ADDRESS]  
**Employee:** [FULL NAME AND ADDRESS]

## 2. Appointment

Job title: [TITLE]  
Start date: [DATE]  
Continuous employment date: [DATE]  
Appointment: [permanent/fixed-term/temporary]  
Probation: [3/6] months, extendable by [PERIOD] where reasonably required.

The Employee reports to [ROLE] and will perform duties reasonably consistent with the appointment.

## 3. Place of work

Normal place: [ADDRESS/REMOTE/HYBRID]. Reasonable travel may be required to customer, project or field locations. Any overseas work terms must be confirmed separately before travel.

## 4. Hours

Normal hours: [HOURS AND DAYS], with reasonable additional hours where necessary subject to working-time law. Breaks and any on-call arrangements are [DETAILS].

## 5. Pay

Salary: £[AMOUNT] gross per [year/month], paid [monthly] on or around [DATE], subject to tax and deductions. Any bonus, commission or pay review is [discretionary/contractual as specified]. A pay review does not guarantee an increase.

## 6. Holiday

Holiday entitlement: [DAYS] plus [public holidays/inclusive wording], pro-rated for part-time or partial years. Holiday year: [DATES]. Booking, carry-over and payment rules are in the holiday policy and applicable law.

## 7. Sickness and other leave

Sickness reporting and evidence requirements are in the sickness policy. Entitlement is [SSP only/company sick pay details]. Statutory family, parental, bereavement and other leave applies as required by law. Any enhanced schemes are described in current policies.

## 8. Pension and benefits

Pension arrangements: [DETAILS], subject to auto-enrolment law and scheme rules. Other benefits: [DETAILS]. Non-contractual benefits may be changed or withdrawn following appropriate process.

## 9. Expenses

Reasonable authorised business expenses will be reimbursed under the expenses policy against suitable evidence.

## 10. Confidentiality

The Employee must protect confidential information belonging to the Employer, customers and suppliers, including source code, architecture, security information, credentials, pricing, product plans, customer data and telecoms network information. Confidential information may be used only for authorised duties. This duty continues after employment ends.

## 11. Intellectual property

Intellectual property created in the course of employment or using Employer resources, relating to the Employer's business or reasonably anticipated business, belongs to the Employer to the fullest extent permitted by law. The Employee will promptly disclose relevant work and sign documents needed to confirm ownership. Moral rights are waived where legally permitted.

## 12. Data protection, monitoring and security

The Employee must follow privacy, security, acceptable-use, access-control and incident-reporting policies. Employer systems may be monitored proportionately for security, compliance, support and business-continuity purposes in accordance with applicable law and notices. Personal data and customer data must not be placed in unapproved systems or AI tools.

## 13. Equipment and records

Employer property, accounts, records, files and credentials must be protected and returned on request or termination. Business records created during employment belong to the Employer.

## 14. Conflicts and outside work

The Employee must disclose conflicts of interest. Outside work that competes, creates a conflict, risks confidential information, affects performance or breaches working-time obligations requires prior written approval.

## 15. Policies, training and collective agreements

Applicable policies include disciplinary, grievance, equality, anti-harassment, health and safety, data protection, security, absence and expenses. Unless expressly stated, policies are non-contractual. Required training must be completed. Collective agreements: [none/details].

## 16. Notice

During probation: [one week] by either party.  
After probation: Employee [one month]; Employer [one month or statutory minimum if greater].

The Employer may make payment in lieu of notice or place the Employee on garden leave where contractually permitted.

## 17. Disciplinary and grievance

The disciplinary and grievance procedures are available at [LOCATION]. Appeals should be made to [ROLE]. These procedures are non-contractual unless stated otherwise.

## 18. Deductions

The Employee authorises lawful deductions for overpayments, excess holiday, unreturned property, agreed training repayments and other sums properly due, subject to applicable law and prior explanation.

## 19. Termination duties

On termination, the Employee must return property, provide passwords and handover information through approved channels, delete Employer data from personal devices where authorised use occurred, and continue complying with confidentiality and IP obligations.

## 20. Post-termination restrictions

Any restrictions must be tailored to the employee's role and reviewed by a solicitor. Insert only restrictions reasonably necessary to protect confidential information, workforce stability and customer connections.

## 21. Changes

The Employer may make reasonable non-material changes after consultation. Material contractual changes require lawful process and written confirmation. Changes to main written particulars will be notified within the applicable legal period.

## 22. Governing law

This contract is governed by the law of England and Wales. The courts and employment tribunals with lawful jurisdiction may hear disputes.
