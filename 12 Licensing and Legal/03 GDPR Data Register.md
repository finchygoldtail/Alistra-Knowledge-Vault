# GDPR Data Register

Stage 13 of the commercial go-live programme. This maps every category of personal or potentially-personal data fibre-gis actually collects and stores, based on direct code inspection (Firestore collections, Storage paths, Cloud Functions) rather than assumption.

**Important scope note — read before using this for anything legal:** the *factual* columns (what data, where it lives, who can technically access it, what's built for export/deletion) are code-verified and can be relied on. The *legal characterization* columns (lawful basis, controller/processor role, retention period) are **not legal advice** — they're a reasonable starting position for a solicitor or DPO to confirm, adjust, or override, per the programme's own rule against inventing legal retention periods or asserting a DPIA conclusion I'm not qualified to make. Every row needing that sign-off is marked.

## Company details

fibre-gis operates as a **data processor** for its customer businesses (each "business" in the data model is a distinct client organisation — telecoms/fibre contractors) and as a **data controller** for its own limited operational data (its own staff accounts, the platform-owner bootstrap list). This split needs a solicitor's confirmation, not just this document's say-so — but it's the standard model for a B2B SaaS platform holding customer-controlled operational data, and it matches what the code actually does: each business's data is siloed by `businessId`, access is governed by roles the *customer* assigns, and fibre-gis (the vendor) does not decide what gets collected or how long a customer's data should be kept.
