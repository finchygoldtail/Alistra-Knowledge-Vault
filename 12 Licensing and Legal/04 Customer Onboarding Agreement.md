# Customer Onboarding Agreement

> **Draft for solicitor review.** Complete this alongside the Order Form and implementation plan.

## 1. Objective

The parties will configure and launch the contracted AlistraGIS environment for [CUSTOMER] under the agreed hosting model.

## 2. Scope

Included onboarding activities:

- discovery and requirements confirmation;
- organisation, project and role configuration;
- identity and authentication setup;
- approved data migration or import;
- storage and hosting configuration;
- user and administrator training;
- acceptance testing;
- production launch and handover.

Anything not expressly listed is out of scope and requires a change request.

## 3. Customer inputs

The Customer will provide:

- an authorised project lead and decision-makers;
- accurate user, role, project and data requirements;
- lawful, clean and supported-format migration data;
- access to customer-managed cloud or identity systems where applicable;
- timely testing, feedback and approvals;
- its retention, classification and access-control requirements.

## 4. Data migration

The migration schedule must identify source systems, formats, volume, mapping rules, validation owner and rejected-record process. The Customer remains responsible for source-data accuracy and legality. The Supplier will not silently correct uncertain network, commercial or personal data.

## 5. Security and access

Administrator access will be limited to named personnel. Temporary migration credentials must be time-limited and removed after use. Production data must not be placed in development or demonstration systems unless expressly approved and appropriately protected.

## 7. Acceptance

The Customer will test against agreed acceptance criteria. A material failure must be reported with evidence within [10] business days. Minor defects that do not prevent normal use will be recorded for correction and will not block acceptance.

## 8. Delays

Dates depend on timely Customer inputs and third-party availability. Customer-caused delay may move the schedule and create additional charges. Each party must notify the other promptly of foreseeable delay.

## 10. Go-live and early-life support
