# Software and SaaS Licence Agreement

> **Draft for solicitor review — not ready for signature.**

## 1. Parties and order form

This agreement is between [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME], trading as AlistraGIS (**Supplier**), and the customer named in the Order Form (**Customer**). The Order Form records the products, user numbers, environments, fees, term, hosting model and any special conditions.

## 2. Licence grant

Subject to payment and compliance, the Supplier grants the Customer a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable right during the subscription term for authorised users to access and use the contracted AlistraGIS services for the Customer's internal business operations.

The licence does not transfer ownership of the software, APIs, documentation or intellectual property.

## 3. Authorised users and seats

Only named or otherwise authorised users may access the service. Accounts must not be shared. The Customer must not exceed purchased seats or circumvent concurrent-session, authentication, usage or technical controls. Additional users require an updated Order Form or approved upgrade.

## 4. Permitted use

The Customer may:

- manage its authorised GIS, fibre-network, project, audit, production and operational data;
- use documented APIs within purchased limits;
- export Customer Data in supported formats;
- make reasonable internal copies of documentation.

## 5. Restrictions

The Customer must not, except where law expressly prevents restriction:

- copy, resell, sublicense, rent or commercially exploit the service for third parties;
- reverse engineer, decompile or attempt to obtain source code;
- bypass authentication, licensing, rate limits or security controls;
- conduct unapproved penetration testing;
- upload malware or interfere with availability;
- use the service to build a materially competing product using protected elements;
- remove proprietary notices;
- use AI or automated extraction to reproduce the product's protected structure, workflows or documentation.

## 6. Hosting models

### 6.1 Alistra-hosted

The Supplier manages the contracted application environment and the Supplier-selected infrastructure and subprocessors. Responsibilities are defined in the security schedule, SLA and Data Processing Agreement.

### 6.2 Customer-hosted

The Customer is responsible for its cloud account, infrastructure, backups, keys, network controls, platform charges and administrator activity unless the Order Form expressly allocates an item to the Supplier. The Supplier may require supported versions and reasonable diagnostic access.

### 6.3 Hybrid or customer-selected storage

Where Firebase, Microsoft Azure, AWS or another approved provider is selected, the Order Form must identify the controller, processor roles, tenancy, region, access model, backup owner and exit process.

## 7. Customer Data

The Customer owns Customer Data and grants the Supplier the limited rights needed to host, copy, transmit, back up, secure and process it to provide the services. The Customer warrants that it has lawful authority to provide the data.

The Supplier may use genuinely anonymised and aggregated service telemetry to secure, operate and improve the service, provided it cannot reasonably identify the Customer, its personnel, clients or network assets.

## 8. Supplier intellectual property

All rights in AlistraGIS, APIs, modules, architecture, source code, schemas, interfaces, templates, documentation, know-how and improvements remain with the Supplier or its licensors. Bespoke work is Supplier IP unless the Order Form expressly states that identified deliverables will be assigned after full payment.

## 9. Third-party components

The service may depend on third-party mapping, cloud, authentication, storage, messaging and open-source components. Their availability and permitted use may be subject to separate terms. The Supplier will maintain an appropriate open-source and subprocessor register.

## 10. Updates and changes

The Supplier may deploy fixes, security updates and improvements. Material removal of contracted core functionality will be avoided during a paid term unless required for security, law or third-party dependency changes. Major paid modules or bespoke changes are not included unless stated in the Order Form.

## 11. Support and service levels

Support scope, hours, response targets, exclusions and planned maintenance are set out in the SLA. Response targets are not guaranteed resolution times unless expressly stated.

## 12. Fees, renewal and overages

Subscription fees are payable as stated in the Order Form. Usage beyond included storage, API, mapping, data-transfer, user or support allowances may be charged at published or agreed rates. Renewal pricing and notice periods must be stated in the Order Form.

## 13. Security

Both parties must follow reasonable security practices. The Customer controls its users, role assignments, devices and source data. The Supplier controls the application and Supplier-managed components. Suspected security incidents must be reported without undue delay through the agreed contact route.

## 14. Suspension

Access may be suspended where necessary for security, unlawful use, material breach, non-payment of undisputed invoices or risk to other tenants. The Supplier will use proportionate measures and restore access when the cause is resolved.

## 15. Termination and exit

At the end of the term, the licence ends. The Customer may export supported Customer Data during the stated exit period. Bespoke export, migration or transition assistance may be chargeable. Data deletion follows the retention schedule, backup cycle and applicable law.

## 16. Source-code purchase or escrow

No source-code rights are included. An outright purchase, IP assignment, source-code escrow arrangement or perpetual deployment licence requires a separate signed agreement defining precisely what transfers, excluded background IP, third-party licences, warranties, support, transition and payment.

## 17. Audit

On reasonable notice, the Supplier may request information sufficient to verify licensed user numbers and material compliance. Audits must minimise disruption and protect confidentiality.

## 18. General terms

The [[01 Standard Terms and Conditions]] apply. Where this agreement conflicts with them on software licensing, this agreement takes priority.
