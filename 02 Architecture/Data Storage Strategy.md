# Data Storage Strategy

Firebase Firestore stores structured platform data and Firebase Storage stores uploaded files, evidence, photos and handover artefacts.

## Data Domains

- Companies, users and projects for [[Multi-Tenant Architecture]].
- Assets and topology for [[Assets API]] and [[Topology API]].
- Field records for [[Audits API]], [[Production API]], [[Health and Safety API]], and [[Files API]].
