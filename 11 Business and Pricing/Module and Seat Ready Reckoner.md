# Module and Seat Ready Reckoner

> **Quoting aid.** Every figure excludes VAT and binds AlistraGIS only when written into a signed Order Form. Prices come from [[Client Pricing Pack]]; this page exists so a price can be quoted in a meeting without rebuilding the arithmetic.

## 1. The two things that drive the software price

Everything else — hosting, data, support, onboarding — sits on top of these two.

1. **Which modules** the customer licenses.
2. **How many users** they need.

## 3. Seat pricing

The first **10 users are included in Core**. Users above 10 are charged at the rate for the customer's *total* contracted band.

| Total contracted users | Rate per user above the first 10 |
|---|---:|
| 11–25 | **£30/month** |
| 26–50 | **£25/month** |
| 51–100 | **£20/month** |
| 101–250 | **£15/month** |
| 251+ | Enterprise quote |

AlistraGIS platform-support accounts are excluded by the product's own seat logic and are never billable.

## 7. Projects and areas

Core includes **5 active projects/areas**. Beyond that:

| Item | Price |
|---|---:|
| Additional active project/area | **£75 per month each** |
| Large regional or national allowance | Enterprise quote |

Agree the treatment of archived and read-only projects before enforcing this. A customer should not be charged for keeping history.

## 8. Putting a full quote together

```
Monthly recurring
  = Core (£1,250)
  + selected modules
  + seats above 10 at the band rate
  + additional projects (£75 each)
  + hosting surcharge (Model B £850 / Model C £450)
  + support uplift (Priority £500 / Enterprise from £1,250)
  + fixed API or AI fees

One-off
  = onboarding (£1,500)
  + data assessment / migration
  + hosting activation (Model B £2,500 / Model C from £4,500)
  + training and bespoke development
```

Then apply the discount, which touches **recurring software only** — never services, activation or pass-through.

## 9. Worked examples

### Small contractor, our hosting

Delivery Essentials, 15 users, Model A, Standard Support.

- Monthly at list: **£2,550**
- Year one at 15% (first client, annual prepay): **£26,010**
- Plus onboarding £1,500 → **£27,510 for year one**

### Mid-size operator, dedicated environment

Full Suite, 50 users, Model B, Priority Support.

### Network operator, own cloud

Full Suite, 100 users, Model C, Priority Support.

- Software: £5,300
- Customer-hosted surcharge: £450
- Priority Support: £500
- Monthly at list: **£6,250** → £75,000/year
- Year one at 15%: **£63,750**

Remember to say that the customer-hosted option is **not live today** and is quoted as a paid implementation project. See [[Data Licensing and Custody Options]] section 5.

## 10. Guard rails

- Do not sell a module the licence engine cannot enforce as a separate entitlement — Maintenance, PIA/Permits, Topology, Management APIs and FRIDAY AI have no dedicated flags yet.
- Do not promise unlimited users, storage, support or AI usage.
- Do not discount below 15% without director approval recorded in the quote file.
- Always state the year-two price in the quote.
- Always state which hosting model and who pays the cloud bill.
